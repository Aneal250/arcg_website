A Message from Our General Superintendent

"At the Assembly of Redeemed Church of God (ARCG), we are a family united in Christ. We are committed to creating a welcoming environment where everyone can experience God's love, grow in His Word, and discover their purpose.

Whether you are new to the faith or have walked with the Lord for many years, there is a place for you here. We warmly invite you to worship, grow, and serve with us as we glorify God together.

"Now therefore ye are no more strangers and foreigners, but fellow citizens with the saints, and of the household of God." — Ephesians 2:19 (KJV)
